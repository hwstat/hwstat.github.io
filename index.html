I am a PhD candidate in Statistics at Monash University. My research focuses on Bayesian predictive inference and generalised Bayes, with an emphasis on calibrated uncertainty quantification under model misspecification. My broader interests include simulation-based inference, Bayesian nonparametrics, and applications in financial econometrics.
